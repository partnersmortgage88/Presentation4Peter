3 things I'd tell my 17-year-old self

1

Learn how to talk to people.

Communication helps you in every career.

2

Become really good at something.

You don't need to know what it is yet. When you find it, work at it.

3

Build relationships before you need them.

The people you meet today may matter 10, 20 or 30 years from now.

Stay in touch.
